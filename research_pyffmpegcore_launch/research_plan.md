# PyFFmpegCore launch-channel research plan

## Main question

Where can the first public PyFFmpegCore release be shared with relevant Python,
FFmpeg/media-automation, and developer-tool/CI audiences without violating
community rules or making unverified claims?

## Subtopics

1. **Python communities** — identify official or well-governed showcase channels,
   their current self-promotion rules, required disclosure, and the best
   reproducible PyFFmpegCore use case for each.
2. **FFmpeg and media-automation communities** — identify channels where a safe
   task runner is relevant, distinguish support forums from project-showcase
   venues, and capture their current posting rules.
3. **Developer-tool and CI launch channels** — verify the rules for launch or
   show-and-tell venues such as Show HN and developer communities, including
   title/link conventions and anti-spam constraints.

## Synthesis

The findings will be ranked into: release-day channels, channels requiring
prior external activation, and channels to avoid. The final launch kit will
include one factual message per audience, exact evidence links, maintainer
disclosure, and a post-publication verification checklist. No channel will be
marked complete until a real release has actually been shared there.
