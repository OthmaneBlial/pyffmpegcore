# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, code, and other contributions that violate this standard. They may issue a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at `blial.othmane@gmail.com`. Do not use public issues for sensitive conduct reports. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided where possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Good-faith disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; personal attacks are not.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
